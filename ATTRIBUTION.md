# Contributions and attribution

This repository presents Pau Kraus's thesis extensions and findings within a
research project built on existing experimental work and software.

## The research foundation

**Timm Teubner and Sonia Camacho** developed the original human repeated
exchange experiment. Their control-treatment evidence supplies the human
benchmark. The game design and human data are attributed to them.

**Daniel Kral** developed *P1*, the original implementation recreating that
experiment with LLM agents, and supervised the thesis. His software supplies
the experimental foundation, including the base game implementation from which
the thesis's baseline and intervention conditions were run.

**Pau Kraus** extended that implementation for the thesis interventions,
conducted their evaluation, and assembled the final thesis runs, comparisons,
and analysis. The periodic memory–reflection–planning adaptation builds on
the architectural ideas of **Park and colleagues (2023)**.

Full source details are in [the reference list](docs/REFERENCES.md).

## What is included here

The public Python files are standalone scripts written for this showcase:
plotting the aggregate tables, comparing means, illustrating the MRP update
schedule, and checking those calculations. The figures are generated by these
scripts. They present thesis results obtained using the extended research
implementation; their labels credit that experimental foundation.

The aggregate result tables preserve the provenance of the LLM thesis runs and
the separate human experiment. This repository does not contain P1's simulator
modules, original prompt templates, raw participant records, full model
transcripts, or the thesis manuscript. The example schedule illustrates the
protocol and is not the implementation that generated the experimental data.
